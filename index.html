ABOUT SWASTHPATH

Healthcare guidance made simpler.

SwasthPath is designed to help users understand the appropriate next step when they are unsure about a basic health concern.

🧭

Clear Next Step

Get simple guidance about whether to monitor at home, visit a local healthcare centre, or seek urgent care.

🚨

Emergency Awareness

Potential danger signs are checked during the guidance flow and can trigger urgent escalation.

🌐

Local Language

The platform is designed for English, Hindi and Marathi users.

📍

Nearby Healthcare

Users can search for nearby hospitals, clinics and healthcare facilities.
